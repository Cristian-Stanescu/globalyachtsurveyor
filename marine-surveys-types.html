Comprehensive inspection and advisory services for buyers, owners, insurers, and lenders.

Marine Survey Services

  • Pre-Purchase Marine Surveys

    This is the most comprehensive inspection of a vessel and is strongly advised when purchasing a new or used vessel.

  • Insurance Marine Surveys

    This survey is meant to be a general overview of the vessel and its systems for insurance underwriting and financing.

  • Tonnage Measurement Surveys

    Approved for MCA Tonnage Measurement Surveys

  • Appraisal Inspections

    These are performed to gather information to justify and determine the fair market of the vessel.

  • Damage Inspections

    The surveyor can be retained by an insurance company to determine the cause of a loss and determine the extent of loss-related damage.

  • Sea Trials

    A sea trial is conducted to determine that the engine operates within the manufacturer's specifications. A rudimentary engine inspection is conducted. The engine is observed for wide-open throttle RPMs, as recommended by the engine manufacturer.

    The condition of hoses, belts, and fuel lines are observed, steering and controls are checked, and if issues are found, recommendations or specialist follow-up are advised.

  • Osmosis Testing

    Used to determine if excess moisture is present in areas of the hull that may not be visible to the eye and to pinpoint potential problem areas.

    Readings can be affected by multiple variables, and in some cases destructive testing may be required to verify meter results.

  • Oil Analysis (Spectrometry)

    This test of engine oil helps determine if excess engine wear is occurring and checks for fuel, engine coolant, and saltwater contamination.

  • Corrosion Analysis

    Bonding, zinc anode protection, and stray current corrosion can be checked to prevent accelerated deterioration of submerged metal components.
