What We Offer

Our Survey Services

Comprehensive marine survey solutions tailored to your needs

Pre-Purchase Survey

A thorough inspection of the vessel before purchase, covering the hull, deck, mechanical systems, electrical systems, and all on-board equipment to ensure you make a fully informed decision.

Learn More

Insurance Survey

Required by most insurers, our insurance surveys assess the current value and condition of your vessel, ensuring you have appropriate coverage at the right price.

Learn More

Appraisal Survey

An independent valuation of your vessel based on current market conditions, condition, and comparable sales — ideal for financing, estate planning, or dispute resolution.

Learn More

Damage Survey

Detailed assessment of damage caused by collision, grounding, fire, flooding, or other incidents. We document all damage and provide cost estimates for repairs.

Learn More

Condition & Value Survey

A combined assessment providing both the current condition of the vessel and its fair market value, offering a complete picture for buyers, sellers, and financiers.

Learn More

Tonnage & Documentation

Official tonnage measurement and documentation assistance for vessel registration, flag state compliance, and legal requirements in various jurisdictions.
